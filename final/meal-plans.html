🗄️ Optimal Storage

The Glass Container System

Invest in airtight glass containers. Not only do they preserve flavor better and prevent chemical transfer, but they also provide a clear view of your weekly inventory, reducing waste.

🍃

Prolonged Freshness

Wash and completely dry leafy greens before storing them with an absorbent paper towel to extend their shelf life.
